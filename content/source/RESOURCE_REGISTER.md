## Rights and attribution rules
- Treat Goethe, DW, VHS and IDS pages as reference sources, not a free content dump.
- Create original German practice questions, dictation scripts, email scenarios and model answers.
- Link to official practice materials rather than embedding copies.
- If a specific source grants an open license, record the exact license and attribution requirements before reuse.
- Do not use copyrighted exam papers as seed data.
- Add a `source_note` field to imported content when a fact or curriculum decision is based on an external source.
